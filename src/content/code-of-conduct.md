# Cocoboko Community Code of Conduct

## Welcome

Welcome to the Cocoboko community—which includes our Discord server, the Cocoboko Cafe—a cozy space to chat, chill, and connect around games, creativity, and community. Before joining the conversation, please take a moment to read and respect these guidelines.

## Be Thoughtful. Be Kind.

We're all human here. That means we bring different backgrounds, experiences, identities, and opinions—and that's a good thing. Treat everyone with **respect**, even when you disagree. Listen more than you talk. Assume good intent. Kindness is not optional.

## Zero Tolerance for Harassment

- Racist, sexist, or ableist language
- Personal attacks or name-calling
- Unwanted DMs, especially for self-promotion
- Jokes that punch down or make others uncomfortable

If you cross the line, we'll act, including bans without warning.

## Keep It Clean & Constructive

- Use appropriate language
- Share content that's safe for work
- Avoid spam, trolling, or derailment
- Respect content warnings and boundaries

## No Politics, No Drama

Our community isn't the place for debating current events or engaging in flamewars. Keep discussion focused on games, creativity, and chill conversation.

## Promote with Permission

Sharing your work? Awesome—we love that. But please only post links or self-promotion in the **designated spaces (like channels on our Discord)** and with intention. No drive-by link drops.

## Moderators Have Your Back

Mods are here to help keep the space welcoming. If you see something concerning or feel uncomfortable, please DM a mod (via Discord) or use the report feature on platforms that support it. We'll listen and act discreetly.

## One Rule Above All

**Respect the human on the other side of the screen.**  
If you're ever unsure, err on the side of empathy.

Thanks for being here. Grab a seat, pour your favorite drink, and let's make something great together.
