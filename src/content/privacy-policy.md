## Website analytics

Cocoboko Studios values your privacy. This website uses **Simple Analytics** to collect anonymous, privacy-first usage data. No personal information is collected, and no cookies or tracking fingerprints are used.

### Information Collection

Simple Analytics collects basic anonymous usage data to help improve the browsing experience. This includes:

- Page views and number of visits
- Time spent on site (session duration)
- Traffic sources and referring sites
- Device type, browser version, and operating system
- Approximate geolocation (country and region only)
- Interaction metrics such as clicks and scroll depth
- Browser and screen settings
- Whether the visitor has Do Not Track (DNT) enabled

### Data Usage

This information is used to:

- Understand visitor behavior
- Gain a general understanding of what types of content resonate with visitors
- Identify technical issues
- Better serve the Cocoboko Studios community

### Privacy Choices

This website respects the "Do Not Track" (DNT) setting in your browser. If DNT is enabled, Simple Analytics still counts your visit, but does not store any identifiable metrics beyond that. You can also use browser settings or extensions to further restrict tracking.

## Email updates (MailerLite)

If you choose to sign up for email updates, we use **MailerLite** to collect and manage your email address and to send occasional emails when there is something worth sharing (for example: a game announcement, demo, or release).

### What we collect

- Your email address
- Subscription metadata (such as the date/time you signed up and confirmation status)

### How we use it

- To send occasional updates you have opted in to receive
- To maintain the mailing list (for example: preventing duplicates and honoring unsubscribe requests)

### How to unsubscribe

Every email update includes an **Unsubscribe** link in its footer. Some email providers may also display an unsubscribe option near the sender information. You can use either option at any time. MailerLite will remove your address from the active mailing list so that you no longer receive newsletters or automated email updates.

If you want your email address removed entirely rather than retained as an unsubscribed record, you can contact us and ask us to delete it from our mailing list.

MailerLite may process this information on our behalf to provide the email service. We do not sell your email address.
